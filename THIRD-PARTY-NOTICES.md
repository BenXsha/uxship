## 4. Node.js dependencies

All runtime and build dependencies use permissive licences only — MIT, Apache-2.0, ISC,
BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, 0BSD, BlueOak-1.0.0. The single MPL-2.0 component
(`lightningcss`) is a **build-time** transitive dependency of Vite and is not redistributed.

The scan covers every dependency tree in this repository; the repository's licence gate re-runs it
and fails on strong copyleft or unknown licences in runtime dependencies.

## 5. Not redistributed: third-party brand logos

A collection of 1,863 company-logo SVGs (upstream `gilbarbara/logos`, CC0-1.0) was previously
shipped in-tree and has been **removed**.

Rationale: CC0-1.0 waives copyright but **does not grant trademark rights** — company logos remain
the property of their respective owners. Where brand logos are wanted, `logo:<name>` is resolved
at runtime through the Iconify API's `logos` collection instead of being redistributed.
